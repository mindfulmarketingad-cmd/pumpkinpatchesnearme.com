Why be listed here

Pumpkin patch demand is compressed into about six weeks, and it is overwhelmingly local and last-minute. People search, scan a map, check whether you are open today, and go. A farm that is missing from the map — or listed with hours from two seasons ago — loses those visitors to whoever is listed correctly.

Being in the directory is free because a complete map is worth more to us than a paywalled one. Featured placement exists for farms that want to stand out during the two or three weekends that make their season.

What we need from you

To create or correct a listing, send us:

  • Farm name and full street address
  • Public phone number and website
  • Season dates and opening hours, including weekend-only periods
  • What you offer: u-pick, corn maze, hayrides, petting zoo, apple picking, food, haunted attractions
  • Admission and pumpkin pricing structure
  • Anything visitors get wrong — parking, cash-only gates, dog policy, stroller access

We do not charge for corrections, ever, and owner-supplied hours override our aggregated data.

Our editorial line

Paid placement is clearly labelled and does not affect anyone's rating, review count or eligibility to be listed. We will not remove a competitor from the map, we will not fabricate reviews, and we will not present a paid placement as an editorial recommendation. If we ever cannot say that honestly, the programme is not worth running.
