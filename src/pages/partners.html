Why claim your listing

Pumpkin patch demand is compressed into about six weeks, and it is overwhelmingly local and last-minute. People search, scan a map, check whether you are open today, and go. A farm that is missing from the map — or listed with hours from two seasons ago — loses those visitors to whoever is listed correctly. Claiming your listing is how you make sure that farm is yours.

Every pumpkin patch we've imported already has a free listing page with an address, hours, ratings and directions — that part costs nothing and always will. Claiming adds verification and priority placement on top of it.

What you get

A claimed listing is marked as owner-verified, moves ahead of unclaimed farms on its state, town and attraction pages, and gives you a direct channel to correct anything in our data — hours, pricing, season dates, what you actually offer. We do not charge separately for corrections once a listing is claimed.

Our editorial line

Claiming a listing verifies ownership and moves a farm up in placement — it does not change anyone's star rating or review count, and it never removes a competitor from the map. We will not fabricate reviews or present a paid claim as an independent editorial recommendation. If we ever cannot say that honestly, the programme is not worth running.
