1. Who we are

This policy covers PumpkinPatchesNearMe.com (the "Site"). Contact us about privacy at {{CONTACT_EMAIL}}.

2. Information we collect

Information you give us

The Site has no accounts, no logins and no server-side form processing. Our contact form composes a message in your own email client — we receive only what you choose to send us by email, which will include your email address and whatever you write. We keep that correspondence for as long as needed to handle your request and to maintain a record of listing corrections.

Information collected automatically

Like nearly all websites, our hosting provider records standard server and security logs, which may include your IP address, browser type, referring page, pages requested and timestamps. Third-party services described below also collect information automatically.

Location

If you press Use my location on the map, your browser asks your permission and, if granted, provides your approximate coordinates to your own browser so the page can sort results by distance. That location is used in your browser only. We do not transmit it to our servers and we do not store it. Denying the permission simply means you search by ZIP code instead.

ZIP code searches

When you search a ZIP code that is not already in our dataset, your browser sends that ZIP code to a third-party postal-code lookup service (Zippopotam.us) to convert it into map coordinates. Only the ZIP code is sent.

3. Cookies and similar technologies

We do not set our own tracking cookies. Third parties operating on the Site may set cookies or use similar technologies:

  • Google AdSense and its partners may use cookies and device identifiers to serve and measure advertising, including personalised advertising where permitted.
  • Map, tile and font providers receive requests from your browser when map tiles, scripts or fonts load, which necessarily discloses your IP address and user agent to them.

You can control cookies through your browser settings. Blocking them will not break the directory.

4. Advertising

We display advertising to fund the Site, including advertising served by Google AdSense.

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this Site and other sites on the internet.
  • You can opt out of personalised advertising by Google at Google Ads Settings.
  • You can opt out of personalised advertising by many other vendors at aboutads.info and networkadvertising.org.

5. Third-party services we use

  • Google AdSense — advertising delivery and measurement.
  • OpenStreetMap and Esri World Imagery — street and satellite map tiles.
  • Zippopotam.us — ZIP code to coordinate lookup.
  • Google Fonts — web fonts.
  • Our hosting provider — serving the Site and keeping security logs.

Each of these has its own privacy policy governing what it does with the data it receives. We do not control their practices.

We use the limited information we receive to respond to your messages, add and correct listings, operate and secure the Site, understand aggregate traffic patterns, and display advertising.
