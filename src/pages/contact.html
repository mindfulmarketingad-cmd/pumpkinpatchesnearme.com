Adding a pumpkin patch

Listings are free. To get a farm added quickly, include:

  • Farm name and full street address, including ZIP code
  • Public phone number and website
  • Season dates and opening hours
  • What is on offer: u-pick pumpkins, corn maze, hayrides, petting zoo, apple picking, food, haunted attractions
  • Admission and pumpkin pricing

Correcting a listing

Tell us the listing name and what is wrong — hours, address, phone number, or a farm that has closed. Corrections are free and are not conditional on any paid placement. Owner-supplied hours take priority over our aggregated data.

Removal requests

If you own a listed business and want the listing removed, email us from an address associated with the business and we will remove it. We do not require a reason.

Press and partnerships

For advertising, sponsorship or featured placement, see the partners page first, then get in touch with your farm or brand details and target region.

What we cannot help with

We are an independent directory, not the farms themselves. We cannot book tickets, check whether a specific farm is open today, process refunds, or resolve a complaint about a visit. For any of those, contact the farm directly using the phone number on its listing page.
